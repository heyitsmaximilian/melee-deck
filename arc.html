02 · The idea

Imagine opening a market on anything, the minute it happens.

We're building a new category: a permissionless prediction market. Anyone can open a market on any question and earn from trading fees without needing a market maker.

03 · The pattern

Prediction markets opened to traders one step at a time. Creators are next.

Every step so far widened who could trade. Melee is the first to widen who can create.

08 · Participants

Five kinds of participant make a market.

Every market pays the people who make it. Creators, backers, affiliates and resolvers all earn on every trade.

Creators

Open the market and set their fee.

Traders

Buy, switch outcomes, cash out early.

Resolvers

Settle the market onchain, paid per market.

Affiliates

Bring traders, paid from the creator's share.

Presale backers

Fund the pool before trading opens and earn the most.
