Why only Melee could run it

A curated platform lists a market once a desk agrees to quote it. When this one started, no desk was quoting and no listings team was at work.

Polymarket listed it on day five, after the odds had moved from 22% to 81%. By then the price discovery, the early traders and the creator were all on Melee.
